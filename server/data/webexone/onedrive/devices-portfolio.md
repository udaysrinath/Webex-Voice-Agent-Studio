# 1  Room devices and collaboration boards

Room devices bring video, audio, content sharing, and intelligent camera experiences into shared spaces.

## Cisco Room Bar BYOD

BEST SUITED FOR  Focus rooms, huddle rooms, and smaller spaces that primarily use laptops

WHAT IT IS  A simplified Room Bar package for high-quality USB-C bring-your-own-device meetings, with an upgrade path to a native room experience.

DESCRIPTION  Room Bar BYOD gives smaller spaces a simple way to improve laptop-based meetings. Users connect their laptop over USB-C, while registration to Control Hub adds enterprise management and workspace insight.

## Cisco Room Bar

BEST SUITED FOR  Huddle rooms and small meeting rooms

WHAT IT IS  An integrated video bar with camera, microphones, speakers, and the processing needed for an intelligent room experience.

DESCRIPTION  Room Bar is an all-in-one video device for smaller rooms. It combines high-quality audio and video, content sharing, intelligent framing, and flexible meeting-platform support in a system that is easy for IT to deploy and manage.

## Cisco Room Bar Pro

BEST SUITED FOR  Medium meeting rooms

WHAT IT IS  A more powerful integrated video bar with dual-camera intelligence, enhanced audio, broader connectivity, and support for larger room setups.

DESCRIPTION  Room Bar Pro expands the video-bar experience for medium spaces. It adds stronger camera, audio, screen, and accessory capabilities while retaining the simplicity of an integrated, centrally managed device.

## Cisco Board Pro G3

BEST SUITED FOR  Small and medium rooms that need meetings and interactive ideation

WHAT IT IS  An all-in-one collaboration board with a 55- or 75-inch touch display, dual cameras, microphones, speakers, whiteboarding, and an NVIDIA-powered computing platform.

DESCRIPTION  Board Pro G3 combines intelligent video meetings and interactive whiteboarding in one touch-enabled device. It is a strong fit when teams want to meet, share, annotate, and create together in the same space.

## Cisco Room Kit EQ

BEST SUITED FOR  Medium and large rooms requiring a modular design

WHAT IT IS  A modular room kit combining a powerful codec, intelligent camera options, Room Navigator, and support for external displays and peripherals.

DESCRIPTION  Room Kit EQ is designed for rooms that need more flexibility than an integrated video bar. It lets organizations select displays, cameras, microphones, and other peripherals while keeping a consistent Cisco room experience.

## Cisco Room Kit EQX

BEST SUITED FOR  Flagship medium-to-large rooms

WHAT IT IS  An integrated room solution that packages Room Kit EQ technology, directional audio, flexible mounting, and external displays into a polished room design.

DESCRIPTION  Room Kit EQX delivers a premium, integrated experience for important collaboration spaces. It combines powerful camera and audio intelligence with a clean, repeatable design that is easier to deploy at scale.

## Cisco Room Kit Pro G2

BEST SUITED FOR  Large, complex, and high-impact spaces

WHAT IT IS  A next-generation modular room platform with extensive connectivity, scalable audio and video over IP, advanced multi-camera experiences, and Codec Pro G2 processing.

DESCRIPTION  Room Kit Pro G2 is built for the most demanding collaboration spaces. It supports advanced cameras, microphones, multiple displays, AV-over-IP designs, and cinematic room experiences while simplifying management through Cisco software.

INTEGRATED OR MODULAR?  Room Bars and Boards package the main experience into an integrated device. Room Kits provide more flexibility to select displays, cameras, microphones, and room peripherals for specialized spaces.

# 4  Room controls, microphones, and cameras

Accessories extend the room experience and help Cisco devices fit different layouts and collaboration needs.

## Cisco Room Navigator

BEST SUITED FOR  Room control, booking, wayfinding, and workspace experiences

WHAT IT IS  A 10-inch touch device available for table or wall placement, used to control meetings, book spaces, show room availability, and run supported workplace applications.

DESCRIPTION  Room Navigator is the touch point for controlling and booking Cisco rooms. Depending on how it is deployed, it can sit inside the room, show availability outside the room, or support workplace experiences such as wayfinding and desk booking.

## Cisco Table and Ceiling Microphones

BEST SUITED FOR  Rooms that need wider or more precise voice pickup

WHAT IT IS  A family of table and ceiling microphones, including IP-based Pro models with intelligent processing and integration with Cisco camera experiences.

DESCRIPTION  Cisco microphones extend clear voice pickup beyond the device itself. Table options suit many conventional rooms, while ceiling and IP-based Pro models support cleaner layouts, larger coverage, and more advanced room designs.

## Cisco Quad Camera

BEST SUITED FOR  Medium and large rooms using modular Room Kits

WHAT IT IS  A multi-lens camera bar that frames and tracks people across a room and supports intelligent multi-participant views.

DESCRIPTION  Quad Camera gives medium and large rooms intelligent framing and speaker tracking from one integrated camera bar. It is commonly paired with Cisco Room Kits for inclusive, scalable room deployments.

## Cisco Room Vision PTZ

BEST SUITED FOR  Large rooms, divisible spaces, classrooms, and advanced multi-camera designs

WHAT IT IS  An IP-based pan-tilt-zoom camera with AI-enhanced tracking and video-over-IP deployment for cinematic RoomOS experiences.

DESCRIPTION  Room Vision PTZ adds flexible, intelligent camera coverage for more complex spaces. It can support presenter tracking, extended speaker views, cross-view, and other multi-camera experiences when paired with compatible Cisco room systems.

## Cisco PTZ 4K Camera

BEST SUITED FOR  Large rooms, learning spaces, auditoriums, and custom camera placements

WHAT IT IS  A premium 4K pan-tilt-zoom camera for extended reach, presenter tracking, and flexible integration with Cisco room systems.

DESCRIPTION  PTZ 4K Camera provides powerful zoom and flexible placement for spaces where a fixed camera is not enough. It is well suited to presenters, stages, classrooms, and larger rooms.

# RoomOS and Control Hub

## RoomOS

BEST SUITED FOR  The experience running on supported Cisco collaboration devices

WHAT IT IS  Cisco's workspace operating system, providing meeting controls, intelligent audio and video, whiteboarding, room functions, integrations, and ongoing feature delivery.

DESCRIPTION  RoomOS is the intelligence inside Cisco collaboration devices. It creates a consistent way to meet, share, co-create, and use workspace features across personal devices and shared rooms.

## Control Hub

BEST SUITED FOR  IT teams deploying and managing devices and workspaces

WHAT IT IS  The cloud management experience for provisioning, configuration, software, analytics, device health, troubleshooting, and workspace insight.

DESCRIPTION  Control Hub helps IT manage Cisco devices and spaces at scale. It provides visibility into device health, usage, configuration, software, and service quality without requiring an administrator to be in the room.

# Common attendee questions

## Which device is best for my room?

SUGGESTED ANSWER  It depends mainly on room size, number of participants, displays, meeting platforms, and whether you want an integrated or modular design. I can give you a general starting point and connect you with a devices specialist for a precise recommendation.

## Can Cisco devices join other meeting platforms?

SUGGESTED ANSWER  Yes. Cisco devices support a range of native and interoperable experiences, including Webex, Microsoft Teams Rooms, Zoom Rooms, Google Meet, and laptop-based BYOD depending on the product, configuration, and release stage.

## What is the difference between Room Bar and Room Bar Pro?

SUGGESTED ANSWER  Room Bar is designed primarily for huddle and small rooms. Room Bar Pro adds more camera, audio, display, connectivity, and accessory capacity for medium spaces.

## When would I choose a Board?

SUGGESTED ANSWER  Choose a Board when touch, whiteboarding, annotation, and visual ideation are central to the space. It combines those experiences with the camera, audio, and meeting system in one device.

## When would I choose a Room Kit?

SUGGESTED ANSWER  Choose a Room Kit when the space needs more flexibility—such as selected displays, external microphones, multiple cameras, unusual layouts, or a larger-room AV design.
