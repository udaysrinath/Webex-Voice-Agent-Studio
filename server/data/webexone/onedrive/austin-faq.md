# General information

Q: Where is WebexOne taking place?

A: WebexOne takes place at Fairmont Austin, 101 Red River Street, in downtown Austin. The hotel is near the Austin Convention Center, Lady Bird Lake, Rainey Street, and downtown dining and entertainment.

Q: Is Fairmont Austin the official WebexOne hotel?

Q: What is Austin known for?

A: Austin is known for live music, food and drink, creative culture, outdoor recreation, and distinctive neighborhoods. Attendees can combine downtown attractions with lakefront trails, museums, music venues, shopping, and local dining.

Q: What is close to Fairmont Austin?

A: Lady Bird Lake, the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail, Rainey Street, Waterloo Greenway, the Austin Convention Center, and downtown restaurants are nearby. South Congress, East Austin, Zilker Park, Moody Center, and ACL Live are better reached by rideshare or other transportation.

Q: What should attendees pack for Austin in October?

A: Bring comfortable walking shoes, breathable layers, sun protection, and a reusable water bottle. Austin weather can change quickly, so attendees should check the current forecast before arriving and before outdoor plans.

Q: How should attendees stay safe while exploring Austin?

A: Use the same awareness you would in any major city: stay with your group when possible, use well-lit routes at night, keep valuables secure, and confirm transportation details before getting in a vehicle. Call 911 for emergencies.

# Getting to and around Austin

Q: How do I get from Austin Bergstrom International Airport to Fairmont Austin?

A: Austin-Bergstrom International Airport is approximately 10 miles from Fairmont Austin. Rideshare, taxis, rental cars, private transportation, and Capital Metro are available. The airport directs arriving rideshare and taxi passengers to the pickup area under the Consolidated Rental Car Facility, near the Red Garage. Check the airport ground transportation page for current pickup instructions.

Q: Can I use public transit from the airport?

A: Yes. Capital Metro Route 20 serves Austin-Bergstrom International Airport and downtown Austin. Use the CapMetro Trip Planner or app for current routes, fares, and service times.

Q: Can I walk around from Fairmont Austin?

A: Yes. Much of downtown, Rainey Street, Lady Bird Lake, and the Austin Convention Center are walkable from the hotel. For South Congress, East Austin, Zilker Park, Moody Center, ACL Live, and most attractions outside downtown, a rideshare is usually more practical.

Q: Where can I find Fairmont Austin parking information?

A: Use the WebexOne Venue and Travel Guide or Fairmont Austin directly for current self-parking, valet, overnight, and oversized-vehicle rates. Parking details can change, so the Concierge should not quote rates unless they have been recently verified.

Q: How do I get to Moody Center from Fairmont Austin?

A: Fairmont Austin describes Moody Center as a short rideshare away. Its Moody Center guide says most guests reach the venue in about 10 minutes by rideshare, depending on traffic and event timing. Confirm your route and departure time before leaving.

Q: Are there accessibility resources at the airport?

A: Austin-Bergstrom International Airport provides accessibility resources, including a Mobility Assistance Zone for travelers using mobility devices or needing additional mobility support. For airline wheelchair service, contact the airline in advance.

# Neighborhoods and things to do

Q: What can I do near Lady Bird Lake?

A: Walk, run, or bike along the Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail; enjoy skyline views; or arrange a permitted kayak, canoe, or stand-up paddleboard rental. Swimming, wading, and bathing in Lady Bird Lake are prohibited. Always check current trail conditions and rental availability before heading out.

Q: Is there anything to know before using the Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail?

A: The trail is a popular 10-mile shared-use loop around Lady Bird Lake. As of September 2026, the City of Austin reports a north-shore reroute near the Confluence and Austin Rowing Club building that is expected to continue through March 2027. Check the City of Austin closures page before you go.

Q: What is Rainey Street?

A: Rainey Street is a nearby entertainment district on the edge of downtown and Lady Bird Lake. It is known for historic bungalows repurposed as bars and restaurants, patios, cocktail spots, and nightlife. It is an easy option for attendees looking for a casual evening near the hotel.

Q: What is South Congress?

A: South Congress, often called SoCo, is a popular south-Austin district for local shopping, restaurants, coffee, street art, and skyline views. It is best reached from Fairmont Austin by rideshare.

Q: What is East Austin?

A: East Austin offers restaurants, bars, galleries, independent shops, and a mix of food and nightlife options. It is a good choice for attendees who want to explore beyond downtown. Use a rideshare and confirm current venue hours before you go.

Q: Where can I experience Austin live music?

A: Austin has live music across the city, including clubs, theaters, restaurants, and concert venues. Use the Visit Austin music and venue guides to find current performances and choose a venue that matches your schedule and music preferences.

Q: What are good outdoor options?

A: Lady Bird Lake and the Butler Trail are convenient downtown choices. Zilker Metropolitan Park is a larger destination with Barton Springs Pool, Zilker Botanical Garden, trails, and other recreation facilities. Check official park pages for current access, hours, reservations, and closures.

Q: What are classic Austin attractions?

A: For a blend of culture and local character, consider a live show at Continental Club, exhibits at the Blanton Museum of Art, or a visit to the Texas State Capitol. For outdoor time, choose Lady Bird Lake, Barton Creek Greenbelt, or Barton Springs Pool. Confirm hours, tickets, and access with the official attraction before visiting.

Q: Where should I eat or get a drink?

A: Downtown, Rainey Street, South Congress, and East Austin each offer many food and drink options. The WebexOne Venue and Travel Guide also highlights Franklin Barbecue, Torchy’s Tacos, and Odd Duck as examples of Austin dining. Recommend the neighborhood that matches the attendee’s preferred setting, cuisine, price point, and travel time. For a specific restaurant, confirm hours, reservations, and accessibility directly with the venue.

Q: Where can I shop in Austin?

A: For independent boutiques and local finds, consider North Loop or South First Street. For a larger open-air shopping destination, consider The Domain. Confirm current store hours and transportation before visiting.
