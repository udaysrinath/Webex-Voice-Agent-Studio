# Webex Customer Awards 2026

## Overview

The Webex Customer Awards recognizes companies and individuals who are excelling at hybrid work, customer experience, and AI with Webex. The 2026 winners will be announced live at WebexOne.

Important: This knowledge source lists award finalists. It does not identify winners.

## Closing Keynote: Webex Customer Awards

Date: Thursday, October 8

Join us for the 5th Annual Webex Customer Awards ceremony.
