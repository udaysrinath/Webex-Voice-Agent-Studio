How do I choose my specific training labs and classes?

Instructions:

- Log in to the Technical Training Agenda Builder using your registration email and password.

- Browse sessions by date, track, or topic to find classes and labs that match your interests and schedule, or search for the title of the class or lab.

- Click a session name to read the full abstract, see who’s speaking, and review additional session information.

- Click Add to Schedule to add a class or lab to your personal agenda.

- Waitlist: Beginning September 16th, if a class or lab is full, click Add to Waitlist.

  - Waitlisting does not guarantee access.

  - Joining a waitlist blocks that time slot on your personal training agenda.

  - Waitlisting prevents you from adding another class or lab during that same time slot, unless you leave the waitlist. You may leave the waitlist at any time to free up your slot. If space becomes available, you’ll be automatically added and notified by email based on waitlist order.

  - New waitlist additions close Sunday, October 4th at 1 pm ET / 6 pm BST.

- Dropping a class or lab from your agenda will remove you from that session and release the seat for other attendees.

5-minute grace period for class and lab attendance: Many labs and some classes have full enrollments, with full waitlists.  Please plan to be seated in your selected labs and classes on time. 5 minutes after the scheduled start time of the session, any open seats will be offered to attendees on the waitlist for the sessions.

What are Capture the Flag labs?
Capture the Flag (CTF) labs are gamified, self-directed learning environments designed to provide hands-on technical experience in aligning to WebexOne collaboration outcomes. They offer a walk-in format where participants can join during designated training hours, select a technical lab of their choice, engage in learning and building activities, and earn points. The program is flexible, allowing learners to fill gaps in their schedule or explore if they missed a scheduled lab. At the end of the program, participants who score highest on the leaderboard can win prizes, though the labs are also valuable for those simply looking to enhance their skills.
