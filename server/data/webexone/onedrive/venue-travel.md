Parking & Transporation
WebexOne attendees can receive a 10% parking discount.

Hotel guests: Let the Fairmont Austin front desk know you are attending WebexOne with Cisco. The 10% parking discount will be applied to your hotel folio.

Non-hotel guests: Visit the Registration & Information Desk to request a parking discount voucher.

Self-parking hourly rates:

- 0–3 hours: $25 (plus tax), $10 per hour thereafter

- Overnight: $55 (plus tax)

- Valet parking hourly rates:

- 0–3 hours: $36 (plus tax), $10 per hour thereafter

- Overnight: $68 (plus tax)

- Oversized vehicles: $75

The Fairmont Austin does provide EV charging stations for guests. These are typically managed through the valet parking service. We recommend checking with the valet staff upon arrival for availability and specific charging rates.

Nearby daily parking public options include the City garages at 201 East 2nd Street and 601 East 5th Street. The Austin Convention Center is closed for construction through 2028, but its parking garages remain open. These public options do not include overnight parking.

Reaching downtown Austin is easy with many ground transportation options, from rideshares and car rentals to taxis and public transit.

<Learn More> https://www.austintexas.gov/department/getting-around-austin

Transportation & Parking

CapMetro Public Transit
Catch a ride with CapMetro, the public transportation provider in Central Texas. Download the CapMetro app to buy passes and plan trips on the go. CapMetro provides a network of bus routes to keep people moving into and out of downtown and throughout the city.

Getting to and from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport
Find information on ground transportation options to and from the airport, including taxis, rideshare, shuttles, pedicabs, electric low speed vehicles, limousines, and charter services.

Bicycling
Riding a bike is a fun, healthy, and environmentally friendly way to get around Austin. Visit the Bicycle Program webpage for information, including route maps and safety tips.

Bike Share
Download the CapMetro Bike Share app to rent an electric bike and get around central Austin without having to buy a bike of your own.

Downtown Parking
Visit the Parking Enterprise webpage for information about parking downtown, including the "Affordable Parking Program", parking meter enforcement times, a map of downtown parking lots and garages, and even a mobile app to assist you in finding a downtown parking spot.

Downtown Parking Map
View the Downtown Parking Map for available parking garages and surface lots in the downtown area.

Upcoming Event Map
Visit the Upcoming Special Event Street Closures webpage for a map of which streets may be affected by upcoming events. The map displays event routes but may not include all expected street closures and affected areas. The map is for informational purposes only and is subject to change at any time.

Exploring Austin

While you’re here for WebexOne, take some time to explore all that Austin has to offer—whether it’s catching live music, discovering local flavors, or unwinding in the city’s welcoming atmosphere.

## Live Music

Austin City Limits, an iconic music festival held in Zilker Park, is taking place directly before and after WebexOne. Extend your trip and stay for the live music. But note, hotel rooms in the area may be limited so plan ahead.

## Dining

Austin’s culinary landscape is as eclectic as the city itself. Savor legendary Texas barbecue at Franklin Barbecue, enjoy inventive tacos at Torchy’s, or experience farm-to-table flavors at Odd Duck. For a special night out, explore the acclaimed restaurants in the bustling downtown or along South Congress Avenue, each offering a taste of Austin’s dynamic food culture.

## Attractions

Immerse yourself in Austin’s creative energy with a live show at the historic Continental Club, discover inspiring exhibits at the Blanton Museum of Art, or take in the beauty of the Texas State Capitol. Outdoor enthusiasts can kayak on Lady Bird Lake, hike the Barton Creek Greenbelt, or cool off with a swim at Barton Springs Pool. No matter your interests, Austin offers a variety of attractions to explore beyond the conference.

## Shopping

Austin’s shopping scene is as fun and diverse as the city itself. Browse independent boutiques in the North Loop district, hunt for vintage finds on South First Street, or explore the open-air shops at The Domain. Whether you’re seeking handcrafted goods, unique souvenirs, or high-end brands, Austin’s shops offer something for every style.
