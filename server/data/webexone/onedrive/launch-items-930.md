# Event narrative

WebexOne 2026 frames the event around “Connected Intelligence: Building the workplace of today, for the workforce of tomorrow.” The portfolio presents a shift from using AI to working with AI: people, assistants, and agents share context and move work forward across collaboration, customer experience, and the physical workplace.

AI that knows your work: context follows people across conversations and helps them prepare, understand priorities, and find what matters.

AI that gets work done: assistants, agents, skills, and connectors can complete supported tasks and multi-step workflows.

AI that works alongside you: agents can join spaces, meetings, and calls as teammates, while people stay responsible for judgment and decisions.

In customer experience, Cisco Dialog aims to move beyond linear journeys to a continuous relationship, with persistent context, long-running execution, rapid onboarding, and learning within enterprise policies.

In the workplace, RoomOS brings intelligence into rooms and devices; Cisco Cloud Control, AI Canvas, and AI Agent 360 extend visibility, action, and governance for IT and agent operators.
