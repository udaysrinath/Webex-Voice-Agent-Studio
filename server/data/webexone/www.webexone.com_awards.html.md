## Frequently asked questions.

#### Who can nominate a customer for a Webex Customer Award?

We encourage customers to self-nominate. In addition, Cisco employees and partners can submit a nomination on behalf of a customer with the customer’s consent.

#### Can I submit more than one nomination for the same company?

Yes, you can! Just make sure that you submit a separate nomination form for each award category and ensure that each nomination form only includes content specific to that award.

#### Are Cisco Partners eligible to submit for the Webex Customer Awards?

The Webex Customer Awards are for Webex customers only, unless specified in the award. We encourage Partners to nominate their customers.

#### How are winners selected?

Please see each award category for specifics. In general, submissions that include measurable outcomes and real-world impact of Webex collaboration or customer experience solutions will be ranked most competitively.

#### Is this program limited to a specific geographic region?

The Webex Customer Awards program is a global program, and we welcome submissions from all regions.

#### Do the Webex Customer Awards recognize a company or an individual at the company?

The Webex Customer Awards recognize both the company and individuals. The Webex Luminary award recognizes individuals while the other categories recognize a company.

#### When is the deadline to submit a nomination?

The deadline to submit nominations is July 10, 2026 at 11:59PM CT.

#### Does the customer need to be registered for WebexOne 2026 to be nominated for an award?

The customer does not need to be registered for WebexOne to be nominated, but finalists must attend the conference in person to be eligible to win.

#### How will the winner for the Dynamic Duo: People’s Choice category be selected?

The winner will be chosen through a popular vote by attendees registered for WebexOne 2026. Only registered attendees will be eligible to vote.

#### What do I get as a Webex Customer Awards finalist?

Finalists will receive a complimentary WebexOne 2026 Conference Pass (excludes Training).

#### Do I need to attend WebexOne 2026 to win?

Yes, all award finalists must attend WebexOne 2026 in person to be eligible to win.

#### When and where will the winners receive their awards?

The winners will be publicly announced at [WebexOne 2026](https://www.webexone.com/) at the Fairmont Austin hotel. The awards ceremony will be a live, in-person event on October 8 at WebexOne 2026.

#### What will a winning organization or individual receive?

Winners will receive a trophy and will be featured in a series of Webex Customer Awards blog articles. The winner may also be featured in other marketing or promotional materials.

#### Who can I contact if I have questions?

Please contact [meltang@cisco.com](mailto:meltang@cisco.com) with any questions regarding the Webex Customer Awards.
