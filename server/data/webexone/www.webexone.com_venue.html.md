Traveling to Austin

### Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (AUS)

Located approximately 10 miles from the Fairmont Austin, Austin-Bergstrom International Airport is the closest and most convenient airport to the event venue.

![](https://www.webexone.com/content/dam/www/us/en/images/webexone/2026/venue/traveling-to-austin/austin-airport.jpg/_jcr_content/renditions/cq5dam.web.1280.1280.jpeg)

### Parking & Transporation

Parking at Fairmont Austin is available at the following rates:

Self parking hourly rates:

- 0-3 hours: $25 (plus tax) $10 per hour thereafter
- Overnight: $55 (plus tax)

Valet parking hourly rates:

- 0-3 hours: $36 (plus tax) $10 per hour thereafter
- Overnight: $68 (plus tax)
- Oversized vehicles: $75

Reaching downtown Austin is easy with many ground transportation options, from rideshares and car rentals to taxis and public transit.

Exploring Austin

While you’re here for WebexOne, take some time to explore all that Austin has to offer—whether it’s catching live music, discovering local flavors, or unwinding in the city’s welcoming atmosphere.

### Live Music

[Austin City Limits](https://www.aclfestival.com/), an iconic music festival held in Zilker Park, is taking place directly before and after WebexOne. Extend your trip and stay for the live music. But note, hotel rooms in the area may be limited so plan ahead.

![](https://www.webexone.com/content/dam/www/us/en/images/webexone/2026/venue/exploring-austin/01-exploring.jpg/_jcr_content/renditions/cq5dam.web.1280.1280.jpeg)

### Dining

Austin’s culinary landscape is as eclectic as the city itself. Savor legendary Texas barbecue at [Franklin Barbecue](https://franklinbbq.com/), enjoy inventive tacos at [Torchy’s](https://torchystacos.com/), or experience farm-to-table flavors at [Odd Duck](https://www.oddduckaustin.com/). For a special night out, explore the acclaimed restaurants in the bustling downtown or along [South Congress Avenue](https://www.austintexas.org/explore/entertainment-districts/south-congress/), each offering a taste of Austin’s dynamic food culture.

![](https://www.webexone.com/content/dam/www/us/en/images/webexone/2026/venue/exploring-austin/02-exploring.jpg/_jcr_content/renditions/cq5dam.web.1280.1280.jpeg)

### Attractions

Immerse yourself in Austin’s creative energy with a live show at the historic [Continental Club](https://continentalclub.com/), discover inspiring exhibits at the [Blanton Museum of Art](https://blantonmuseum.org/), or take in the beauty of the [Texas State Capitol](https://tspb.texas.gov/prop/tc/tc/capitol.html). Outdoor enthusiasts can kayak on [Lady Bird Lake](https://www.austintexas.gov/page/lady-bird-lake), hike the [Barton Creek Greenbelt](https://www.austintexas.gov/department/barton-creek-greenbelt), or cool off with a swim at [Barton Springs Pool](https://www.austintexas.gov/department/barton-springs-pool). No matter your interests, Austin offers a variety of attractions to explore beyond the conference.

![](https://www.webexone.com/content/dam/www/us/en/images/webexone/2026/venue/exploring-austin/03-exploring.jpg/_jcr_content/renditions/cq5dam.web.1280.1280.jpeg)

### Shopping

Austin’s shopping scene is as fun and diverse as the city itself. Browse independent boutiques in the [North Loop district](https://www.austintexas.org/austin-insider-blog/blog/post/north-loop/), hunt for vintage finds on [South First Street](https://www.austintexas.org/austin-insider-blog/blog/post/south-first-street/), or explore the open-air shops at [The Domain](https://www.simon.com/mall/the-domain). Whether you’re seeking handcrafted goods, unique souvenirs, or high-end brands, Austin’s shops offer something for every style.

![](https://www.webexone.com/content/dam/www/us/en/images/webexone/2026/venue/exploring-austin/04-exploring.jpg/_jcr_content/renditions/cq5dam.web.1280.1280.jpeg)
